## 2. The 4 Pillar Capabilities Highlighted

### 🔍 1. Free Read-Only Forever ("The Manifesto")
> *"Reading your campaigns should be free and always will be."*
- **Zero Credit Card Required**: Instant access to read-only tools across all 27 platforms.
- **Audit in Seconds**: Query active campaigns, search term reports, CPA anomalies, and underperforming ad sets directly in Claude Code or Cursor.
- **Fatigue Detection**: Early warning system flagging creative fatigue when CTR drops by >30%.

### 🎨 2. Interactive Creative Canvas
- **Visual Studio Inside Your Workflow**: Generate, inspect, and tweak ad creatives without switching to external graphic design tabs.
- **Multi-Aspect Ratio Support**: Automated framing for 1:1 Feed, 9:16 Vertical Stories/Reels, and 16:9 Landscape banners.
- **AI Asset Generation**: Direct integration with Imagen 4, Flux, and SDXL with CTR scoring predictions.

### 👥 3. Cross-Platform Audiences Engine
- **Unified Cohort Sync**: Build high-intent B2B or mobile gamer cohorts in conversation and sync them across Google Customer Match, Meta Custom Audiences, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, and AppLovin in parallel.
- **First-Party Data Integration**: Connect CSVs, customer lists, and technographic signals (e.g., developers using Claude Code).

### 📊 4. Unified Cross-Channel Reports
- **Single Source of Truth**: End the Monday morning ritual of stitching together 6 disparate CSV exports.
- **Real-Time Cross-Channel ROAS**: Blend revenue and spend across search, social, programmatic, and mobile app networks.
- **Budget Pacing Guardrails**: Real-time pacing telemetry with anomaly alerts to prevent overspending.

## 5. Maker Comment (First Comment by Joel Horwitz)

```markdown
Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I'm Joel, founder of Synter.

Before starting Synter, I led growth marketing at Sourcegraph. At one point, our marketing team underwent severe downsizings, and I was suddenly managing search, social, and programmatic campaigns across 7+ ad networks completely alone.

The hardest part wasn't writing copy or setting budgets—it was the cognitive friction. Every platform had a different interface, different attribution models, and clunky reporting. Just figuring out which ad sets were losing money took hours of CSV exports every Monday morning.

When the Model Context Protocol (MCP) was introduced, everything clicked. Why should growth engineers and media buyers spend hours in disparate web dashboards when an AI agent can query and optimize campaigns directly through conversation?

Today, we're launching **OSS Synter MCP** with our core manifesto:

> **"Reading your campaigns should be free and always will be."**

Here is what we built:

1. **Free Read-Only Auditing**: Full read-only access across 27 ad networks—Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Reddit, and now mobile app networks like **AppLovin**, **LiftOff**, and **Unity**. Zero credit card required, zero vendor lock-in.
2. **The Creative Canvas**: An interactive visual canvas where AI agents generate, inspect, and tweak multi-aspect ratio ad creative (1:1, 9:16, 16:9) before anything goes live.
3. **Cross-Platform Audiences**: Define a high-intent audience once in plain English and sync it across LinkedIn, Meta, Google, and AppLovin in parallel.
4. **Unified Reports**: Real-time cross-channel ROAS, budget pacing, and creative fatigue detection in one place.

### How to get started in 10 seconds:
Add it to Codex:
`codex mcp add synter-ads --url https://mcp.syntermedia.ai --bearer-token-env-var SYNTER_API_KEY`

Add it to Claude Code:
`claude mcp add synter-ads --transport http https://mcp.syntermedia.ai`

Or Cursor / Claude Desktop via npm:
`npx -y @smithery/cli install @synterai/mcp-server --client claude`

Everything is 100% open source on GitHub: https://github.com/jshorwitz/synter-media

I'd love your feedback, questions, and feature requests. What would you build if your AI could query your ad accounts for free? 🙏
```
